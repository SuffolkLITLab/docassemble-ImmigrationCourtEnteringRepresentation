# Immigration Court Entry of Appearance (Form EOIR-28)

This package provides a Docassemble interview that helps authorized representatives complete Form EOIR-28 (Notice of Entry of Appearance as Attorney or Representative Before the Immigration Court).

## Run the interview

Live interview: https://apps.suffolklitlab.org/start/ImmigrationCourtEnteringRepresentation/form_eoir28_omb11250006

Source code: https://github.com/SuffolkLITLab/docassemble-ImmigrationCourtEnteringRepresentation

## Who should use this

Use this interview if you are allowed to represent someone in Immigration Court (attorney, EOIR-accredited representative, law student or graduate, reputable individual without pay, accredited foreign government official, or person authorized to practice before December 23, 1952). Do not use this interview for cases before the Board of Immigration Appeals (BIA). Use Form EOIR-27 instead.

## Before you start

- EOIR eRegistry account (needed to file in ECAS)
- Represented party's name, address, and A-number
- Immigration Court location
- Your EOIR ID number
- Your contact and firm/organization information

## After you finish

- Sign and file the form with the Immigration Court
- Serve a copy on DHS unless you filed in ECAS and the opposing party participates in ECAS
- Keep a copy for your records
